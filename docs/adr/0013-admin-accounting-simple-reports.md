# ADR 0013: Admin and Accounting Simple Reports

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Context

Reports are needed for owner review and Accounting reconciliation, but the first production reporting scope must stay simple. Branch and Stock Staff should use their operational pages and dashboards rather than broad report exports.

## Decision

1. Reports menu access is limited to Admin and Accounting Staff.
2. Reports are read-only.
3. Admin can access Sales, Accounting/Reconciliation, and Inventory reports.
4. Accounting Staff can access Sales and Accounting/Reconciliation reports only.
5. Reports support date presets and custom date range: Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, and from/to dates.
6. Reports export to PDF only. CSV export for reports is deferred. Inventory page can still have Admin CSV export separately.
7. Sales Report includes summary plus receipt-level detail.
8. Accounting/Reconciliation Report includes verification summary plus flagged mismatch detail.
9. Admin Inventory Report includes current stock summary plus movement summary.
10. Simple report charts may appear in the UI only. PDF output contains tables/totals only.
11. Reports run live DB queries every time. No saved report snapshots and no stored PDF files.
12. Cancelled/voided transactions are excluded from active sales totals and report rows. Voided sales remain queryable in the Accounting all-status audit queue.
13. Reports can show Admin-only branch performance. Staff ranking/performance reports are not included.
14. PDF layout includes company header: Chezcar name/logo placeholder, report title, date range, generated by, and generated at.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Reports support Admin review and Accounting reconciliation without exposing broad data to every role.
- Live queries avoid report snapshot/storage complexity.
- PDF-only export matches printable/shareable reporting needs.
- UI charts can help quick review without complicating PDF rendering.

### Negative

- Regenerated reports can differ if underlying data changes.
- No CSV export means spreadsheet analysis requires later work or Inventory-page export.
- No staff performance report means people-management analytics are out of scope.

## Rejected Alternatives

- **Reports for all roles:** exposes broader data than needed for the initial production app.
- **CSV first:** simpler technically, but the selected business need is printable/shareable PDF.
- **Saved report snapshots:** adds storage, lifecycle, and retention concerns before reports are complex enough to need it.
- **Staff ranking:** sensitive and not required for accounting/operations reporting.
