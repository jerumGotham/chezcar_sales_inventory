# Reports Production Spec

**Status:** Five focused reports implemented
**Last updated:** 2026-09-07
**Source:** Owner grill-with-docs decisions; ADR 0017 (supersedes ADR 0013)

## Purpose

Provide five concise, read-only reports from live persisted data: Sales, Inventory Summary, Inventory Movements, Returns & Warranty, and Low Stock. Operational Accounting queues and open Customer Orders remain in their own modules and are not duplicated here.

## Access

- Reports require `reports:view`; PDF export additionally requires `reports:export`.
- Access is determined by capabilities, not built-in role names.
- Every query and export is limited to effective location access. Company-wide users may compare all authorized branches; branch-scoped users see only their branch.
- Sensitive cost fields require a separate cost-view capability if introduced.

## Global Rules

- Reports query the live database and do not save report snapshots or generated PDFs.
- Date-based reports default from the first day of the current month through today.
- Date presets may include Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, and custom from/to dates.
- Inventory Summary and Low Stock are current snapshots and do not use a historical date range.
- UI tables may paginate, but summary totals must use the full filtered dataset and must never be calculated from a display limit.
- PDF export uses the same selected report, filters, totals, and effective location scope as the UI.
- PDF metadata includes Chezcar name/logo placeholder, report title, selected filters/date range, generated by display identity, and generated at.
- PDF is the report export format. CSV report export and saved schedules are deferred.

## 1. Sales Report

The Sales Report is the monthly sales report by default; there is no separate Monthly Sales menu.

### Inclusion and Date Basis

- Include only `VERIFIED`, non-voided sales.
- Group and filter sales by the system verification timestamp.
- Exclude pending/unverified sales from official totals.
- Customer Orders appear only through their completed/posted final sale, not as open orders.

### Filters

- Verification date from/to.
- Authorized branch/location.
- Salesperson.
- Source: Direct Sale or Customer Order.
- Payment method.

### Summary

- Total verified sales.
- Transaction count.
- Units/items sold.
- Total discount.
- Average sale amount.
- Branch rows with transaction count, units sold, sales total, and percentage of authorized grand total.
- Grand total across selected branches.

### Detail

- Verification date.
- Manual receipt number.
- Branch.
- Customer or Guest.
- Salesperson.
- Authenticated encoder.
- Source.
- Units/items.
- Discount.
- Final amount.
- Verification status.

Salesperson output is transaction attribution, not ranking, commission, or Payroll reporting.

## 2. Inventory Summary

### Filters

- Authorized location.
- Product item code/name.
- Category.
- Brand.
- Product status.
- Stock status.

### Summary and Detail

- Product and location counts.
- Item code/name, category, brand, and product status.
- Location.
- On-hand, reserved, quarantined, and available quantities.
- Reorder level and stock status.
- Optional inventory cost/value only when reliable cost data and a dedicated authorization rule exist.

## 3. Inventory Movements

### Filters

- Occurrence date from/to.
- Authorized location.
- Product.
- Movement type.
- Reference number/type.

### Detail

- Occurrence and server-recorded timestamps.
- Product and location.
- Signed quantity and direction.
- Movement type.
- Source reference.
- Authenticated actor.
- Reason/notes.

The report includes receiving, transfers, sales/reversals, Backjob parts issued/returned, quarantine receipts/releases, customer replacements, supplier returns/replacements, repairs, write-offs, and authorized corrections.

## 4. Returns & Warranty

### Filters

- Case date from/to.
- Authorized branch/location.
- Type: Backjob, Customer Warranty, or Supplier Claim.
- Status and resolution.
- Customer, Supplier, Product, Salesperson, or Installer where applicable.

### Summary and Detail

- Total, open, completed, and overdue cases.
- Counts by type, status, branch, and resolution.
- Quarantined quantity represented by filtered cases.
- Recorded supplier refund/credit amounts, clearly labeled as claim tracking rather than ledger totals.
- Case/reference number, original sale/receiving reference, customer/supplier, product/quantity, assigned personnel, status, resolution, target/overdue dates, and linked case.

## 5. Low Stock

Low Stock is a focused current-snapshot action report, not a duplicate date-based report.

### Filters

- Authorized location.
- Product item code/name.
- Category and brand.
- Low Available or Out of Stock status.

### Summary and Detail

- Low-stock and out-of-stock product/location counts.
- Product, location, available quantity, reorder level, shortage quantity, and status.
- `suggestedReorder = max(reorderLevel - available, 0)` as an informational quantity only; automatic purchase orders are deferred.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Reports page and APIs independently enforce view/export capability and effective location scope.
2. The default Sales Report covers current-month verification dates through today.
3. Sales totals contain only verified, non-voided sales and are not truncated by display limits.
4. Sales branch totals reconcile exactly to the authorized grand total.
5. Accounting queues and open-order lists are absent from Reports.
6. Inventory outputs use `available = onHand - reserved - quarantined`.
7. Inventory Summary and Low Stock are current snapshots; Movements and Returns/Warranty honor selected date ranges.
8. Each PDF matches the selected report, filters, scope, and full aggregate totals.
9. No commission, staff ranking, Payroll, general-ledger, or saved-report behavior is implied.
